I'm Shivam Sah — a self-driven AI founder based in Janakpur, building technology that doesn't compromise between power and simplicity. I started writing code not because of a roadmap, but because I saw problems that AI could genuinely fix.

I founded AI Vexara on one idea: that intelligent systems should work for real people, not just demos. The platform has grown into an advanced AI ecosystem spanning education, conversational intelligence, and voice-first interaction.

My work sits at the intersection of deep AI integration and sharp product craft. Whether it's a voice calling agent that handles real phone conversations, a WhatsApp assistant automating hotel operations, or an educational AI that adapts to each student — I build things that ship, scale, and matter.

I think like a founder first. Every technical decision is a product decision. Every product decision is a people decision.
